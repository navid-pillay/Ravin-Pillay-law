About Ravin

How It Works

From charge to resolution

Knowing what comes next makes a hard time easier. Here is how we approach your case.

Protect your rights

We advise you right away on what to say, what not to say, and what your bail and release conditions mean.

Examine the evidence

We review the Crown's disclosure closely, looking for weaknesses, Charter breaches and gaps in the case.

Fight for the best outcome

Whether that means a withdrawal, diversion, a negotiated resolution or a trial, we pursue the result that protects your future.

Arrested or under investigation?

Remember your rights, and speak to a lawyer before you speak to anyone else about the case.

Stay silent

You have the right to remain silent. Be polite, but don't explain or discuss what happened.

Ask for a lawyer

On arrest or detention, you have the right to speak to a lawyer without delay. Use it.
