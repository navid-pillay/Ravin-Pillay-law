About Ravin

A Toronto lawyer who takes your matter personally.

Ravin Pillay is a lawyer licensed by the Law Society of Ontario, serving clients throughout Toronto and the GTA. His practice is built on a simple idea: clients deserve a lawyer who listens carefully, explains their options in plain language, and stays with them from the first conversation to the final result.

When you work with Ravin Pillay Law, you work directly with Ravin — not a rotating cast of staff. That means fewer surprises, faster answers, and advice that reflects the full picture of your situation.
