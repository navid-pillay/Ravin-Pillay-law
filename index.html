About

Ravin Pillay

Barrister & Solicitor

Ravin Pillay is a Toronto criminal defence lawyer and a graduate of Osgoode Hall Law School. He was called to the Ontario bar in 1999 and practises from his office on Queens Quay East, on Toronto's waterfront.

He examines the evidence, holds the Crown to its burden of proof and makes sure your Charter rights are respected. You deal with him directly, from your first call to the end of your case.
